# Wallet

A digital wallet is an application used to store and present items such as payment cards, tickets,
loyalty cards, digital passes, IDs and digital keys.

## Useful Applications

Well-known proprietary implementations of wallet apps are [Google Wallet](https://wallet.google/)
and [Apple Wallet](https://www.apple.com/wallet/).

There currently is no free and open-source application that can fully replace Google Wallet or Apple
Wallet.
Functions such as contactless payment with debit or credit cards, official digital IDs and digital
car, hotel or house keys depend on support by the respective issuer as well as platform-specific
security and integration.

Free and open-source alternatives can therefore mainly replace the handling of barcodes, loyalty
cards, tickets and Passbook (`.pkpass`) files.

- [Catima](/wiki/android/catima.md) is a loyalty card, barcode and ticket manager that works offline
  and also supports Passbook files (`.pkpass`).
- [FossWallet](/wiki/android/fosswallet.md) is a wallet that supports Passbook files (`.pkpass`) as
  well as pass updates and QR-code-based loyalty cards.
- [fWallet](/wiki/android/fwallet.md) is a wallet for transport tickets, discount cards and
  subscriptions which use Passbook files (`.pkpass`).
