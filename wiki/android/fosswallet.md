# FossWallet

[FossWallet](https://github.com/SeineEloquenz/fosswallet) is a free and open-source Passbook wallet
for [Android](/wiki/android.md).

FossWallet covers the digital-pass and parts of the ticket functionality of applications such as
[Google Wallet](https://wallet.google/) or [Apple Wallet](https://www.apple.com/wallet/).
It works with Passbook (`.pkpass` and `.pkpasses`) files.
It also supports synchronizing pass barcodes with compatible smartwatches through
[Gadgetbridge](/wiki/android/gadgetbridge.md).
It does not provide contactless payment with ordinary debit or credit cards and cannot replace
issuer-backed digital IDs or digital car, hotel or house keys.

## Setup

On [Android](/wiki/android.md) FossWallet is available on [F-Droid](/wiki/android/f-droid.md) in a
package called [FossWallet](https://f-droid.org/packages/nz.eloque.foss_wallet/).
