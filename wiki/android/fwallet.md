# fWallet

[fWallet](https://gitlab.com/TheOneWithTheBraid/f_wallet) is a free and open-source wallet
application for transport tickets, discount cards and subscriptions.

fWallet covers parts of the ticket and digital-pass functionality of applications such as
[Google Wallet](https://wallet.google/) or [Apple Wallet](https://www.apple.com/wallet/).
It works with Passbook (`.pkpass`) files.
It does not provide contactless payment with ordinary debit or credit cards and cannot replace
issuer-backed digital IDs or digital car, hotel or house keys.

## Setup

On [Android](/wiki/android.md) fWallet is available on [F-Droid](/wiki/android/f-droid.md) in a
package called [fWallet](https://f-droid.org/packages/business.braid.f_wallet/).
The application is also available for Linux and as a web application.
